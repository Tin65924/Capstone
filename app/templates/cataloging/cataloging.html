Valid ISBN digits

ISBN-13 — 13 digits

The last digit is a check digit (digits weighted alternately ×1 and ×3 must sum to a multiple of 10). Example: 9780306406157

ISBN-10 — 10 characters

The first 9 are digits and the last is a digit or X (weighted check must divide evenly by 11). Examples: 0306406152, 080442957X

Formatting

Hyphens and spaces are ignored, so 978-0-306-40615-7 works the same. Periodicals use an 8-character ISSN instead (e.g. 1234-567X).

A valid check digit proves the format is correct, not that the number was officially assigned to a publication.
