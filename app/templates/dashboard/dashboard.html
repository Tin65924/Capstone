Borrowing Trends

Definition

Daily checkout volume — how many books left the shelves each day over the last 6 days.

How to interpret

Rising lines mean growing demand. Zeros on open days can signal closures or quiet days — compare weekdays with weekdays, not weekends.

How it’s calculated

Loans with a borrow transaction type, grouped by borrow date over the trailing 6 days. Days without loans are shown as 0 so the axis stays complete.

Example

If Monday to Wednesday read 4, 9, then 2 checkouts, Tuesday was the busiest day — worth an extra hand on the desk next Tuesday.
