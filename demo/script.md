# Faculty Demo – Voiceover Script (one slide per page, plain walkthrough)

Single neutral voice. Each page = one slide + one MP3. Plain user language.
No comparisons, no other roles, no can't-do. 5-book limit stated as your own limit.

---

## P01 – Sign-in page (P01_login.png)
This is the sign-in page. To begin, click Sign in with Google and use your school Google account. Only your school account can enter the library system. Once you sign in, the system takes you to the next step.

## P02 – Choosing your faculty account (P02_role_select.png)
Here you set up your account. Select Faculty, then type your 9-digit ID number, for example 123456789, and choose your department from the list. Double-check your details, then press Continue to submit your account for approval.

## P03 – Waiting for approval (P03_pending_approval.png)
After you submit, you will see this waiting screen. It means your account was received and is waiting for approval. You do not need to do anything else here. Just sign in again later, and once approved you will land on your home page.

## P04 – Home page (P04_home.png)
This is the home page. It welcomes you and shows what is new in the library, like recently added books. From the top menu you can move to the catalog or sign in areas. Think of this as your starting point whenever you open the system.

## P05 – Searching the catalog (P05_catalog.png)
This is the catalog, where you look for books. Type a title, an author, or a keyword in the search box and browse the results. You can filter and open any book to see more. Use this page whenever you want to find something to read.

## P06 – Opening a book (P06_bookinfo.png)
This is the book details page. Here you see the title, the author, and whether a copy is available right now. From this page you can borrow the book to take it home, or reserve it to hold your place. Just fill in the small form and confirm.

## P07 – Your profile and overview (P07_profile.png)
This is your profile and overview. At the top you see your name, your ID number, your department, and your phone number. Below that are your live counts: books you currently have, overdue books, and reservations ready for pickup. Your borrowing limit is 5 books. You can also press Edit Profile to update your name, phone number, or department. Your ID number stays the same.

## P08 – Requesting a book (P08_request_book.png)
This is the request page. If the library does not have the book you need, type the title and the author here and send your request. Your sent requests appear below so you can follow their status at any time.

## P09 – Your history (P09_history.png)
This is your history page. It keeps everything in one place: books you borrowed before, your reservations, your requests, and any fines. Come back here whenever you want to review what you have done in the library.

## P10 – Your notifications (P10_notifications.png)
This is your notifications page. Here you receive friendly reminders, such as when a book is almost due, when it is overdue, or when a reserved book is ready for pickup. Check this page regularly so you never miss a return date. Thank you for watching this tour of your faculty account.
